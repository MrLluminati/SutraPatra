# SutraPatra · Initial editorial plan

This is a **topic backlog**, not a list of published or verified articles. Every topic requires source checking, editorial review, and an original reader benefit before publication.

## Publishing priorities

1. Keep the initial focus on three sections rather than creating many empty categories.
2. Prefer articles with a clear reader intent and information that can be verified.
3. Explain differences in traditions rather than presenting any one regional account as universal.
4. For festival dates/muhurt, verify the **year and locality** with appropriate contemporaneous panchang sources; do not invent dates.
5. Do not republish complete third-party translations, photos or recent copyrighted articles without appropriate rights.
6. Add internal links only where they improve the reader's understanding.

## Minimum release checklist

- [ ] Original, informative text with a specific audience question
- [ ] Important factual claims checked with reliable references
- [ ] No copied commercial images or third-party copyrighted text
- [ ] Accurate title, concise description and correct category
- [ ] Attributions, uncertainty and regional differences are clearly stated
- [ ] Links work; spelling and mobile readability reviewed
- [ ] Editorial approval recorded; `draft: false` only when ready
- [ ] Published article visible in the listing and sitemap

Avoid publishing too frequently at the expense of usefulness. The first milestone is a small, trustworthy archive.
