## Publishing priorities

1. Keep the initial focus on three established sections rather than creating empty categories.
2. Prefer evergreen, well-explained articles with a clear reader purpose.
3. Verify all calendar dates against year and locality; do not invent muhurats.
4. Do not republish commercial images, third-party modern translations or lengthy copyrighted passages without rights.
5. Use internal links only when they add value, and record sources at the end of each sourced article.
6. Run `npm run check:content` and `npm run build` before public releases.

## Minimum editorial checklist

- [ ] Original, informative text and an accurate title
- [ ] Significant claims checked against appropriate references
- [ ] Clear attribution, uncertainty and regional distinctions
- [ ] Internal links work and no copyrighted copying
- [ ] Human copyedit and final editorial approval
- [ ] Published entry appears in `/lekh/` and `/sitemap.xml`

Do not publish solely to achieve a daily posting quota.
